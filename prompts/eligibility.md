ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (protocol, amended 2026-10-12). A study is eligible only when it meets every row.

Intervention
  Include: Learners interact with a generative AI tool as part of a learning activity. This covers large language model chatbots, tutors built on large language models, code assistants, and multimodal generative models.
  Exclude: Intelligent tutoring or adaptive systems with no generative model. Predictive analytics. AI used only for grading. AI used only by the teacher to prepare materials.

Comparison
  Include: A separate control group that does not use generative AI. Business as usual, a non-AI tool, a human tutor, or a non-generative technology all qualify.
  Exclude: Single-group pre-post designs. Studies that only compare two generative AI variants.

Design
  Include: Randomized experiments, individual or cluster. Quasi-experiments with a comparison group.
  Exclude: Correlational, survey, case study, and qualitative designs.

Population
  Include: K-12 and higher education students, in formal or informal settings. Preservice teachers learning STEM content count as higher education.
  Exclude: Workplace training, in-service teachers, general adult samples from crowdsourcing panels.

Domain
  Include: Science, mathematics, statistics, engineering, computer science, programming, data science.
  Exclude: Health professions education. Language learning and other non-STEM content.

Outcome
  Include: A quantitative measure of learning, such as achievement, conceptual knowledge, problem solving, or skill performance, collected after the intervention begins.
  Exclude: Studies reporting only perceptions, satisfaction, or usage. Affective outcomes are not extracted for any study.

Data
  Include: Statistics sufficient to compute a standardized mean difference and its variance.
  Exclude: Studies that remain insufficient after one author query.

Dates
  Include: Published January 1, 2023 or later.

Language
  Include: Full text in English. Language is judged at full text, because a record with an English abstract may have a full text in another language.

Publication type
  Include: Journal articles.
  Exclude: Conference proceedings, including full papers. Posters, work in progress, preprints, dissertations, abstract-only records, editorials, reviews.

EXCLUSION CODES (report the first that applies, in this order)
  E1  Intervention is not learner-facing generative AI
  E2  Content is not STEM
  E3  No control group without generative AI
  E4  Participants are not K-12 or higher education students
  E5  No quantitative learning outcome
  E6  Not an empirical study
  E7  Insufficient data after author query (fulltext stage only)
  E8  Duplicate or superseded version
  E9  Full text not in English (fulltext stage only)
  E10 Publication type not eligible
